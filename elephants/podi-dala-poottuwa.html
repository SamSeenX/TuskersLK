Alive Wild Tusker c. 2005 – Present (Aged approx. 19)

Podi Dala Poottuwa

ගල්ගමුවේ පොඩි දළ පූට්ටුවා

Archival Field Dossier

Roaming Territory Reswehera Forest Reserve, North Western Province
Habitat Sanctuary Reswehera and Galgamuwa wild reserve
Geographic Coordinates 8.03 N, 80.25 E
Physical Anatomy & Tusks Young emergent crossed tusks, alert wild temperament, protected by local community guardians
Fate and Cause of Death None. Roaming wild under protective surveillance by the Department of Wildlife Conservation.

Significance and Cultural Legacy

Carries the rare crossed-tusk genetics of the fallen legend Galgamuwa Dala Poottuwa. Symbolizes hope and natural renewal for wild tuskers in the northwestern dry zone.

"Sri Lankan tuskers represent an ancient biological and cultural heritage. Only a rare percentage of Sri Lankan Asian elephant bulls develop majestic ivory tusks, making each individual a living national treasure."
