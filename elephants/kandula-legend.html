Historical Historical Legend 2nd Century BCE (Reign of King Dutugemunu)

Kandula the War Tusker

කන්දුල ඇතු

Registry Record

  • Roaming Territory: Anuradhapura Royal Citadel and Magama Kingdom
  • Habitat & Sanctuary: Ancient Rajarata and Ruhuna royal stables
  • Physical Anatomy: Peerless battle intelligence, immense physical strength, honored with royal ornaments and golden tusk sheaths
  • Geographic Coordinates: [8.3114, 80.4037]
  • Fate & Cause of Death: Honored natural passing after legendary service in uniting ancient Sri Lanka.

Significance & Cultural Legacy

The most famous war elephant in ancient Sri Lankan history, documented extensively in the Mahavamsa. Served as the loyal mount of King Dutugemunu, battering through the fortified iron gates of Vijithapura fortress.

"Sri Lankan tuskers represent an ancient biological and cultural heritage. Only a rare percentage of Sri Lankan Asian elephant bulls develop majestic ivory tusks, making each individual a living national treasure."
