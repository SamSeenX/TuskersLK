Alive Wild Tusker c. 1985 – Present (Aged approx. 41)

Somawathiya Sando

සෝමාවතියේ සැන්ඩෝ

Archival Field Dossier

Roaming Territory Somawathiya National Park, Polonnaruwa
Habitat Sanctuary Somawathiya river basin, Sungavila, and flood plains
Geographic Coordinates 8.1167 N, 81.1667 E
Physical Anatomy & Tusks Massive muscular build, broad forehead, heavy outward-curving tusks
Fate and Cause of Death None. Roaming wild in the Mahaweli flood plains.

Significance and Cultural Legacy

Named Sando for his colossal body mass and broad muscular build. He is one of the most imposing wild tuskers in the Eastern Province, frequently sighted traversing the sacred Somawathiya marshlands and Mahaweli riverbanks.

"Sri Lankan tuskers represent an ancient biological and cultural heritage. Only a rare percentage of Sri Lankan Asian elephant bulls develop majestic ivory tusks, making each individual a living national treasure."
