Deceased Wild Tusker c. 1970 – 2018 (Aged approx. 48)

Walagamba of Kala Wewa

වලගම්බා

Archival Field Dossier

Roaming Territory Kala Wewa and Galgamuwa, North Central Province
Habitat Sanctuary Kala Wewa basin and Kahalla-Pallekele sanctuary
Geographic Coordinates 8.02 N, 80.35 E
Physical Anatomy & Tusks Massive broad skull, thick symmetrical down-pointing tusks, deep ear pigmentation
Fate and Cause of Death Old age complications combined with past gunshot trap injuries.

Significance and Cultural Legacy

Named after ancient monarch King Walagamba, renowned as one of the largest dominant alpha tuskers recorded in the North Central tank basins. His commanding presence and calm intelligence around Kala Wewa influenced tusker genetics across the region.

"Sri Lankan tuskers represent an ancient biological and cultural heritage. Only a rare percentage of Sri Lankan Asian elephant bulls develop majestic ivory tusks, making each individual a living national treasure."
