Deceased Wild Tusker c. 1967 – 2017 (Aged approx. 50)

Galgamuwa Dala Poottuwa

ගල්ගමුවේ දළ පූට්ටුවා

Registry Record

  • Roaming Territory: Galgamuwa and Sorabora Reserve, North Western Province
  • Habitat & Sanctuary: Galgamuwa, Reswehera, and Siyambalangamuwa forests
  • Physical Anatomy: Distinctive criss-crossed tusks, imposing stature, known for peaceful coexistence with rural farmers
  • Geographic Coordinates: [8.0125, 80.2825]
  • Fate & Cause of Death: Poached by ivory traffickers with high-powered rifle fire in November 2017. His tragic demise ignited nationwide outrage and strict anti-poaching wildlife legislation.

Significance & Cultural Legacy

Sri Lanka most recognizable wild tusker with unique crossed tusks forming an X shape. Despite suffering from blindness in one eye due to past trap gun injuries, he roamed the Galgamuwa dry zone peacefully for decades.

"Sri Lankan tuskers represent an ancient biological and cultural heritage. Only a rare percentage of Sri Lankan Asian elephant bulls develop majestic ivory tusks, making each individual a living national treasure."
