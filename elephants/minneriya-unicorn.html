Deceased Wild Tusker c. 1980 – 2019 (Aged approx. 39)

Unicorn of Minneriya (Ekadanta)

මින්නේරියේ යුනිකෝන්

Registry Record

  • Roaming Territory: Minneriya National Park & Moragaswewa, North Central Province
  • Habitat & Sanctuary: Minneriya tank catchment and Kaudulla wildlife corridor
  • Physical Anatomy: Single massive, sharp, upward-curving left tusk, dominant matriarchal escort, towering back ridge
  • Geographic Coordinates: [8.0333, 80.9]
  • Fate & Cause of Death: Succumbed to severe gunshot wounds sustained outside the protected park boundary in 2019.

Significance & Cultural Legacy

The iconic single-tusked dominant bull (Ekadanta) of the great Minneriya Gathering. Recognized immediately by wildlife enthusiasts across the world for his solitary, razor-sharp ivory tusk and undisputed leadership around the Minneriya tank.

"Sri Lankan tuskers represent an ancient biological and cultural heritage. Only a rare percentage of Sri Lankan Asian elephant bulls develop majestic ivory tusks, making each individual a living national treasure."
