Deceased Temple Custodian 1953 – 2022 (Aged 69)

Nadungamuwa Raja

නැදුන්ගමුවේ රාජා

Archival Field Dossier

Roaming Territory Nadungamuwa, Gampaha and Kandy
Habitat Sanctuary Nadungamuwa Estate, Gampaha and Sri Dalada Maligawa
Geographic Coordinates 7.2906 N, 80.6337 E
Physical Anatomy & Tusks Over 10.5 ft shoulder height, two parallel floor-grazing tusks, calm and dignified demeanor
Fate and Cause of Death Natural causes due to old age in March 2022. Declared a National Cultural Treasure by Presidential decree.

Significance and Cultural Legacy

The revered 10.5-foot tall gentle giant who carried the sacred Casket of the Tooth Relic at the Kandy Esala Perahera for 15 years. He walked over 90 km along national highways from Gampaha to Kandy flanked by military escort and thousands of devotees.

"Sri Lankan tuskers represent an ancient biological and cultural heritage. Only a rare percentage of Sri Lankan Asian elephant bulls develop majestic ivory tusks, making each individual a living national treasure."
