Deceased Wild Tusker c. 1978 – 2020 (Aged approx. 42)

Kalthota Tusker (Mountain Escarpment Giant)

කල්තොට ඇතා

Archival Field Dossier

Roaming Territory Kalthota, Balangoda and Uda Walawe, Sabaragamuwa
Habitat Sanctuary Kalthota escarpment, Walawe river gorges, and Uda Walawe border
Geographic Coordinates 6.6167 N, 80.8333 E
Physical Anatomy & Tusks Thick curved tusks with ground-sweeping tips, muscular forequarters, elusive nature
Fate and Cause of Death Severe gunshot wounds sustained during human-wildlife encounters along agricultural boundaries in Balangoda.

Significance and Cultural Legacy

The fabled mountain-foothill wild tusker of the southern central escarpment. Adapted to traversing steep rocky slopes between Uda Walawe and the Horton Plains foothills, inspiring local folk tales.

"Sri Lankan tuskers represent an ancient biological and cultural heritage. Only a rare percentage of Sri Lankan Asian elephant bulls develop majestic ivory tusks, making each individual a living national treasure."
