Deceased Wild Tusker c. 1975 – 2022 (Aged approx. 47)

Barana of Kala Wewa

බරණ

Archival Field Dossier

Roaming Territory Kala Wewa, Galkiriyagama, North Central Province
Habitat Sanctuary Kala Wewa wetlands and Kahalla-Pallekele sanctuary
Geographic Coordinates 8.045 N, 80.502 E
Physical Anatomy & Tusks Heavy thickset tusks, prominent shoulder crest, ruler of the tank shores during dry season gatherings
Fate and Cause of Death Succumbed to severe gunshot injuries sustained during human-elephant conflict skirmishes in 2022.

Significance and Cultural Legacy

A dominant wild patriarch tusker of the Kala Wewa basin. Renowned among rangers and wildlife enthusiasts for his bold presence and companionship with tusker Revatha.

"Sri Lankan tuskers represent an ancient biological and cultural heritage. Only a rare percentage of Sri Lankan Asian elephant bulls develop majestic ivory tusks, making each individual a living national treasure."
