Alive Temple Custodian c. 1980 – Present (Aged approx. 44)

Indiraja of Dalada Maligawa

ඉන්දිරාජා

Archival Field Dossier

Roaming Territory Sri Dalada Maligawa, Kandy
Habitat Sanctuary Sri Dalada Maligawa elephant stables, Kandy
Geographic Coordinates 7.2936 N, 80.6413 E
Physical Anatomy & Tusks Tall stature, elegant symmetric tusks, high crown, steady and composed walking gait
Fate and Cause of Death None. Living and serving at the Temple of the Tooth.

Significance and Cultural Legacy

Gifted in 1987 to the Sri Dalada Maligawa by former Indian Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi. Indiraja is one of the senior ceremonial tuskers trusted to carry the Sacred Relic Casket at the annual Kandy Esala Perahera.

"Sri Lankan tuskers represent an ancient biological and cultural heritage. Only a rare percentage of Sri Lankan Asian elephant bulls develop majestic ivory tusks, making each individual a living national treasure."
