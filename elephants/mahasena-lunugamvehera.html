Alive Wild Tusker c. 1980 – Present (Aged approx. 44)

Mahasena of Lunugamvehera

ලුණුගම්වෙහෙර මහාසේන

Archival Field Dossier

Roaming Territory Thirappane and Maradankadawala, North Central Province
Habitat Sanctuary Thirappane and Maradankadawala forest corridors
Geographic Coordinates 8.225 N, 80.5167 E
Physical Anatomy & Tusks Long curving ivory tusks, resilient spirit, recognized by wildlife trackers across Anuradhapura tanks
Fate and Cause of Death None. Surviving under continuous veterinary care after recovering from severe gunshot wounds inflicted in 2023.

Significance and Cultural Legacy

Beloved icon of the Rajarata wilderness. Survived debilitating trap gun and rifle wounds to his front leg thanks to an unprecedented multi-month medical mobilization by wildlife veterinarians and loving villagers.

"Sri Lankan tuskers represent an ancient biological and cultural heritage. Only a rare percentage of Sri Lankan Asian elephant bulls develop majestic ivory tusks, making each individual a living national treasure."
