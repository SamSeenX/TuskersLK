Terms of Service

Last revised: October 2026

1. Educational & Conservation Purpose

The content on Wanali is published exclusively for conservation awareness, biological research, cultural documentation, and educational purposes. All information is compiled from historical chronicles, public records, and wildlife documentation.

2. Responsible Use of Geographic Information

Users agree not to utilize any regional range or location data published on this website for commercial safari harassment, unauthorized tracking, or harmful disruption of wild elephant herds.

3. Intellectual Property & Citations

Historical narratives, ecological descriptions, and registry schemas may be quoted or referenced for academic and non-commercial conservation endeavors provided Wanali is cited as the source.

4. Disclaimer of Liability

Wild elephant encounters carry inherent dangers. Wanali does not encourage close approach or interaction with wild elephants. Always adhere to the regulations and guidance established by the Department of Wildlife Conservation Sri Lanka.
