Critical Tusker Habitats & Havens

Kala Wewa Basin

The ancient tank bed created by King Dhatusena is Sri Lanka premier wild tusker haven. Roamed by Revatha and Barana, it remains a critical seasonal feeding ground rich in aquatic vegetation.

Galgamuwa & Reswehera

Dry-zone scrub jungle renowned for rare crossed-tusk genetics. Homeland of the fallen martyr Galgamuwa Dala Poottuwa and the young emerging Podi Dala Poottuwa.

Temple of the Sacred Tooth (Kandy)

The spiritual home of Sri Lanka most revered ceremonial tuskers. Held the majestic processions of Nadungamuwa Raja, Heiyantuduwa Raja, and currently Indiraja.
